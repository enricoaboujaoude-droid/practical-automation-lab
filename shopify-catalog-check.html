Find catalog risks before they become product-feed problems.

PAL Catalog Check is a read-only Shopify catalog-readiness scanner. It reviews product and variant data, groups findings into critical issues and warnings, and gives merchants clear remediation guidance without editing the store.

What the app checks

Product completeness

Titles, vendor/brand presence, Online Store URLs and product images.

Image readiness

Observable image dimensions are checked against the 500×500 readiness target used in current Merchant Center preparation.

Variant identifiers

Flags variants with neither SKU/MPN nor barcode/GTIN, without inventing identifiers.

Price and variant structure

Flags non-positive prices and duplicate option combinations that can create feed or catalog ambiguity.

Current scan boundaries

  • Up to 2,500 products per scan.
  • Up to 100 variants per product.
  • Up to 20 images per product.
  • The app warns when a scan boundary is reached.

This is a catalog-readiness check, not a guarantee of approval by Google Merchant Center or any other sales channel. The app does not connect to a merchant's Google Merchant Center account.

Current availability

Technical development validation is complete in a Shopify development store, including install, authentication, real catalog scanning, repeat scans, persistent sessions across redeploys, and privacy-compliance webhook support. Public Shopify App Store installation is not open yet; the app is being prepared for external review and merchant beta.

Clicking the beta button records only one aggregate shopify_beta_interest event before opening the public GitHub beta discussion. It does not include store credentials, catalog data, or personal information.
