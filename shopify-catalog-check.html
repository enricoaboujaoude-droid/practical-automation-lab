Make your Shopify catalog easier for AI shopping systems to understand.

PAL AIEO is a read-only Shopify catalog scanner for AI commerce discoverability. It reviews the product identity, attributes, variants, price, availability, images and URLs that AI assistants and shopping agents use to understand and compare products, then gives clear remediation guidance without editing the store.

What the app checks

AI-readable product identity

Titles, vendor/brand, product URLs and images so machine systems can identify the exact product reliably.

Image readiness

Observable image dimensions are checked against the 500×500 readiness target used in current Merchant Center preparation.

Variant identifiers

Flags variants with neither SKU/MPN nor barcode/GTIN, without inventing identifiers.

Price, availability and variant clarity

Flags pricing and duplicate-option ambiguity that can make AI comparison and recommendation less reliable.

Current scan boundaries

  • Up to 2,500 products per scan.
  • Up to 100 variants per product.
  • Up to 20 images per product.
  • The app warns when a scan boundary is reached.

This is an AIEO/catalog-readiness check, not a guarantee of approval, ranking or recommendation by Google, Shopify, ChatGPT or any other AI or sales channel. The app does not connect to a merchant's Google Merchant Center account.
