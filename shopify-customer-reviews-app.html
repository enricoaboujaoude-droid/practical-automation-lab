Collect Shopify customer reviews without adding manual follow-up work

A useful review workflow asks at the right time, avoids repeatedly contacting the same customer, and makes it easy to route feedback to the review platforms your store actually uses.

What to evaluate in a Shopify review collector

  • Trigger timing: request a review after the customer has had enough time to receive and use the product.
  • Order filtering: avoid sending review requests for cancelled, refunded, or inappropriate orders.
  • Volume controls: match the plan to monthly order volume instead of paying for unused capacity.
  • Review destinations: choose whether the workflow should support Google, Trustpilot, or other review destinations relevant to the store.
  • Branding and deliverability: evaluate sender/domain options once review-request volume becomes material.

Before installing any review app

Check your monthly order volume, which review platforms matter to your customers, how long fulfillment normally takes, and whether an existing email/SMS workflow already sends review requests. Avoid overlapping automations that contact the same buyer twice.
