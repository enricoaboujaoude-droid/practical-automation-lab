Privacy

The current tools are designed to perform their core calculations or feed processing in your browser. Practical Automation Lab does not intentionally require you to create an account or submit calculator inputs or product-feed contents to use these tools.

The Product Feed Preflight Auditor sends only five privacy-safe activation event names to a Practical Automation Lab event collector: audit started, audit completed, report downloaded, commercial CTA viewed, and commercial CTA clicked. The collector stores only the event name, whether the record is a synthetic measurement test, and the server timestamp. It does not receive or store CSV contents, filenames, product identifiers, row values, audit findings, or other merchant catalog data.

The hosting provider and other infrastructure providers may process standard technical information such as IP addresses, request metadata and server logs as part of operating, measuring and securing the site.

If analytics, affiliate tracking, advertising, payments or other third-party services are added in the future, this page will be updated to describe those services and the relevant data practices before they are relied on for monetization.
