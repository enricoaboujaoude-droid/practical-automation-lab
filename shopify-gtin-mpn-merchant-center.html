Fast diagnostic order

1. Check the exact variant, not only the parent product

Identifier problems often hide at variant level. Confirm the affected variant has the SKU/MPN or barcode/GTIN you intended to submit.

2. Do not invent identifiers

Use manufacturer-assigned identifiers when they exist. Google’s product-data guidance says not to guess or manufacture an MPN or GTIN.

3. Check related catalog fields at the same time

Review title, vendor/brand, product URL, images, price and variant structure. A feed problem can be compounded by more than one catalog-readiness issue.

4. Then inspect the feed or app sync

If Shopify contains the correct identifier on the exact variant but Merchant Center still receives a blank or different value, the remaining problem may be in the feed mapping, channel app, supplemental source or sync path. PAL does not diagnose Merchant Center transport or account-side configuration.

What PAL Catalog Check can verify

  • Variants where neither SKU/MPN nor barcode/GTIN is present.
  • Product titles and vendor/brand presence.
  • Online Store URLs and product images.
  • Prices and duplicate variant option combinations.
  • Observable image-dimension readiness.

What it cannot prove: that Google received the field, that a particular feed mapping is correct, or that Merchant Center will approve the product.
