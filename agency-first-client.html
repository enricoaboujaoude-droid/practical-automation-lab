Your first agency client starts with a real problem, not a logo.

Before you create a course, buy ads or promise growth, learn to diagnose one business problem and offer a small outcome you can actually deliver. This is an original, practical playbook for beginning with e-commerce merchants.

Zero ad spend requiredService-firstNo income guarantees
One example of genuine market demand: Merchants publicly ask how to resolve Merchant Center disapprovals, mismatched product prices, missing variants and inconsistent landing-page data. These questions show a problem exists; they do not prove the requester is willing to pay.

Seven steps from problem to first paid engagement

01 / RESEARCH

Choose one narrow buyer

Start with a Shopify merchant who has a specific catalog or Merchant Center feed problem. Do not target “everyone who needs marketing.”

02 / EVIDENCE

Verify the pain

Find a first-party description of the issue. Ask permission before inspecting private account data. Confirm that product data—not an unrelated policy, payment or logistics problem—is actually in scope.

03 / DIAGNOSIS

Check what is observable

Compare product-page title, price, currency, availability, variant and image with source feed values. Identify discrepancies that can be reproduced; separate confirmed defects from hypotheses.

04 / OFFER

Define the paid deliverable

Offer a bounded audit with a written list of findings, severity, reproducible evidence and proposed corrections. Specify what you can deliver, turnaround, price and exclusions before taking payment.

05 / CONSENT

Respond where invited

Answer public requests with useful information and transparent affiliation. Seek permission for a quotation; avoid unsolicited DMs, scraped contact lists, mass email or deceptive urgency.

06 / DELIVERY

Fulfill, then measure

Do not take paid orders when your application, checkout or backend cannot deliver. On a funded agreement, provide the stated report, confirm the buyer received it and record accepted corrections.

07 / SCALE

Teach proven methods

After real client outcomes exist, document repeatable processes, consented case studies and lessons learned. Paid coaching comes after evidence and demand—not before.

What should a first audit check?

  1. Product identity: clear title, description, brand, required identifiers and variation grouping.
  2. Transaction consistency: same currency, visible price and availability across feed, product page and checkout.
  3. Fulfillment truthfulness: policies, shipping claims, actual delivery ability and contact information match reality.
  4. Image and category quality: accurate main photo and relevant product categorization.
  5. Evidence quality: a screenshot or machine-readable row supports each specific reported issue.

Google's Merchant Center data specification requires accurate information and consistency between feeds, pages and checkout. An audit can identify issues but cannot guarantee approval, ranking, placement or reinstatement.

Before promising a conversion lift: record a baseline

A merchant can see different order counts in store reports and advertising dashboards because they answer different attribution questions. This alone does not prove a broken pixel or an incremental sale. Before proposing a paid measurement audit:

  1. With the merchant's permission, select one seven-day date range and one timezone; record paid orders separately from reported attributed conversions.
  2. In Shopify Analytics → Reports → Marketing, record the attribution model and relevant channel results. Compare like-for-like date ranges; do not add platform-credited conversions together as if they were unique orders.
  3. Check a consenting example order's Conversion summary when available. If visit details are absent, mark the source unknown rather than guessing. Share a written baseline before claiming improvement.

These checks help define an honest audit scope; they cannot establish causal lift or guarantee sales. Sources: Shopify marketing reports and order conversion summaries.

قبل الوعد بتحسين المبيعات: حدّد فترة سبعة أيام ومنطقة زمنية واحدة، وقارن الطلبات المدفوعة مع التحويلات المنسوبة للإعلانات كلٌّ على حدة. سجّل نموذج الإسناد المستخدم، ولا تجمع أرقام المنصّات كأنها طلبات فريدة. إذا لم تتوفّر بيانات مصدر الطلب، اكتب «غير معروف» بدل التخمين. لا يمكن لهذه المقارنة وحدها إثبات زيادة المبيعات بسبب حملة إعلانية.

ملخّص الدرس بالعربية

قبل أن تبدأ وكالة تسويق رقمي، اختر مشكلة واضحة عند تاجر فعلي، وتحقّق منها بمعلومات دقيقة وبموافقة صاحب المتجر. قدّم تقريراً محدداً وقابلاً للتنفيذ، واشرح السعر وحدود الخدمة قبل استلام أي دفعة. لا تعد بنتائج إعلانية أو أرباح مضمونة، ولا تنشر شهادات زبائن لم تحصل عليها فعلاً.

ابدأ بالخدمة الحقيقية، وثّق النتائج بعد موافقة العميل، ثم حوّل الخبرة المكتسبة إلى محتوى تعليمي أصلي.
