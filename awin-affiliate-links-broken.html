Priority checklist

  • Verify the destination is still the intended merchant or product page.
  • Check redirect chains for stripped or rewritten tracking parameters.
  • Review older editorial pages after merchant program, domain, or catalog changes.
  • Fix the highest-traffic and highest-purchase-intent links first.

How to prioritize repairs

Rank suspected links by traffic × purchase intent × expected commission. Repair the pages most likely to be costing money before low-traffic archive links.
