What to check first

  • Redirect integrity: the destination still exists and lands on the intended merchant or product.
  • Tracking integrity: affiliate parameters survive redirects and do not get stripped.
  • Program migrations: merchants that moved networks or replaced an old program.
  • Expired campaigns: seasonal or launch-specific URLs that are no longer valid.
  • High-intent pages: buying guides, comparisons, reviews, and pages already generating clicks.

Prioritize by expected loss

Fix links where traffic × purchase intent × expected commission is highest. A broken tracking link on a comparison page usually matters more than dozens of broken links in low-intent archive posts.
