Preflight EUDR DDS V3 data before it reaches TRACES.

Validate selected DDS XML/JSON, supplier CSV data and GeoJSON locally in your browser. The checker focuses on technical V3 contract risks: activity type, HS headings, quantities, producer country/geolocation, grouped references and V1/V2 migration leftovers.

Timing: the EUDR applies from 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators and from 30 June 2027 for most micro and small operators. This tool is a technical preflight only. It does not determine legal scope, deforestation-free status, country risk, or whether due diligence is legally sufficient.

    What this checks

    • DDS V3 namespace / service endpoint signals and V1/V2 leftovers such as operatorType, ddsIdentifier, associatedStatements, TRADE and legacy volume.
    • Allowed activityType values: IMPORT, EXPORT, DOMESTIC.
    • At least one commodity, maximum 100 commodities, HS heading syntax (2–6 digits), description and quantity signals.
    • For IMPORT / EXPORT, netWeight presence; positive quantity values; supplementary-unit qualifier pairing.
    • Producer country two-letter format and geometryGeojson presence where producer data is supplied.
    • Base64-encoded GeoJSON readability, supported geometry types, coordinate range and six-decimal precision signals.
    • Grouped reference / verification number length constraints where those fields are present.

    This is not a full XSD/business-rule engine and cannot inspect facts that are absent from the input. A clean result does not guarantee acceptance by the EUDR Information System or compliance with Regulation (EU) 2023/1115.
