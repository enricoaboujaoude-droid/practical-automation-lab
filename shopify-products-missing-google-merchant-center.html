October 2026 check: did the Merchant API migration affect the feed?

Google's Content API for Shopping sunset on August 18, 2026. If products, custom labels or market segments changed around the transition, investigate the data-source side before editing every Shopify item.

  1. In Google Merchant Center, open Settings → Data sources and identify the active Shopify-connected source. Note the source and country/market labels.
  2. Check whether product attribute rules (custom labels, shipping labels, promotion IDs) exist on the current source. Rules on older sources may not carry to newly generated country data sources.
  3. Compare one missing variant's item ID, country/language label, availability, landing URL and price with the product data actually processed by Merchant Center.
  4. If Shopping campaigns filter by an old feed label, verify they also include the new country-code data-source labels. Do not assume a missing ad is a missing product.
  5. In Shopify's Google & YouTube channel, confirm the expected Merchant Center account, markets and product publication settings; allow for processing before judging the outcome.

Google's own guidance discusses multi-market data-source labels and rules and the Merchant API product model. The checklist is educational, not a claim that PAL can repair merchant account rules.

If the catalog looks clean

Move downstream. Check whether the product is eligible for the intended sales channel, whether the Google/YouTube or feed app is sending the expected variant, whether a supplemental feed or rule is overriding data, and whether Merchant Center is still processing or rejecting the item.

PAL’s boundary: PAL Catalog Check inspects Shopify catalog readiness. It does not connect to Merchant Center and cannot prove why Google omitted a product.

Why run PAL before changing the feed?

It gives you a bounded read-only snapshot of the Shopify-side problems PAL knows how to detect. If PAL finds catalog issues, fix and rescan. If it finds none, you have stronger evidence to focus on the feed, sync or Merchant Center side instead.
