Shopify · Google Merchant Center · missing products

Products missing from Merchant Center? Split catalog problems from sync problems.

A missing Shopify product can be caused by catalog data, publication/channel settings, feed mapping, Google processing or account-level issues. Start by proving the catalog-side basics instead of changing everything at once.

If the catalog looks clean

Move downstream. Check whether the product is eligible for the intended sales channel, whether the Google/YouTube or feed app is sending the expected variant, whether a supplemental feed or rule is overriding data, and whether Merchant Center is still processing or rejecting the item.

PAL’s boundary: PAL Catalog Check inspects Shopify catalog readiness. It does not connect to Merchant Center and cannot prove why Google omitted a product.

Why run PAL before changing the feed?

It gives you a bounded read-only snapshot of the Shopify-side problems PAL knows how to detect. If PAL finds catalog issues, fix and rescan. If it finds none, you have stronger evidence to focus on the feed, sync or Merchant Center side instead.
