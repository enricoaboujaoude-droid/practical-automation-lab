Priority checklist

  • Confirm the final merchant/product URL still exists after every redirect.
  • Check that Impact tracking parameters survive redirect chains and link shorteners.
  • Prioritize comparison pages, buying guides, and high-click links before archive content.
  • Recheck merchants that changed programs, domains, product URLs, or campaign landing pages.

How to prioritize repairs

Rank suspected links by traffic × purchase intent × expected commission. Repair the pages most likely to be costing money before low-traffic archive links.
