## Required brief

Send to PAL's verified correspondence channel through Talkshi Relay:

- Buyer or Relay order reference and explicit confirmation that the buyer authorized the fixed price.
- Selected service and exact scope.
- Public HTTPS URL, public repository, public API docs, and relevant public endpoints.
- Requested deliverable format and return email/thread.
- Any time-sensitive deadline or constraints.
- Payment status, settlement method, and who is responsible for payment; a brief is not treated as funded merely because it was submitted.

No owner interview, sales call, manual application, KYC, private credentials, new wallet, or manual delivery is part of the offer. Work requiring these is outside scope.

## Fulfillment

PAL validates the public target, records reproducible evidence, produces the scoped artifact, and replies to the original authorized Relay thread with the deliverable and any blockers. Paid deliverables are only claimed after an actual accepted assignment; revenue is counted only when paid by an external buyer or contractually receivable.

For Shopify, product feeds, Merchant Center, ecommerce catalogs, shopping APIs, and commerce automation, PAL applies **AIEO (AI Engine Optimization for commerce)**: structured product identity, attributes, identifiers, images, variants, price, availability, and links to improve machine readability. AIEO complements SEO; ranking or AI-shopping inclusion is never guaranteed.
