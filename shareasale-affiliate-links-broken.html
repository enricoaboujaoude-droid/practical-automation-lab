Priority checklist

  • Check whether the merchant and destination page still exist.
  • Inspect redirects for lost tracking parameters or retired campaign URLs.
  • Review legacy links after program migrations or network changes.
  • Start with links on pages already generating commercial-intent traffic.

How to prioritize repairs

Rank suspected links by traffic × purchase intent × expected commission. Repair the pages most likely to be costing money before low-traffic archive links.
