# PAL Catalog Check — publication economics and spend controls

Checkpoint: 2026-09-17

## Authorized spend

The owner has authorized one narrowly scoped pre-revenue expense:

- Shopify App Store registration: up to **$19 USD one time**

Authorization condition:

- all autonomous/no-cost submission preparation must be complete
- the registration fee must be the final meaningful blocker to Public distribution

No other paid pre-revenue expense is authorized by this decision.

## Current Shopify economics

Shopify's current official documentation states:

- App Store registration is a one-time $19 USD fee per Partner account
- developers retain 100% of the first $1,000,000 USD in gross app revenue under the current revenue-share model
- app billing is subject to a 2.9% processing fee and applicable sales tax

These terms must be re-checked immediately before registration and before activating paid plans because platform rules can change.

## Recovery rule

The $19 registration fee is not assumed or represented as guaranteed to be recovered.

The internal break-even objective is:

> observable net PAL Catalog Check app revenue attributable to real merchants must exceed the actual registration fee and relevant processing/tax effects.

Examples, ignoring tax solely for illustration:

- $19 gross app revenue less 2.9% processing is about $18.45, so one $19 payment would not fully recover a $19 registration fee.
- $29 gross app revenue less 2.9% processing is about $28.16, which would exceed a $19 registration fee before tax effects.

This does not set final pricing. Pricing must follow merchant-value and willingness-to-pay evidence, not the desire to recover a small sunk cost.

## Portfolio discipline after publication

Track separately:

- registration spend
- public installs
- first scans
- repeat scans
- commercial/paid-plan interest
- gross app revenue
- processing/platform charges
- observable net revenue
- cumulative net revenue after registration cost

Do not call the fee recovered until the measured cumulative net revenue is positive after the registration cost.

If App Store publication does not produce sufficient exposure or activation, fix distribution/onboarding before judging demand. If exposure is adequate but recurring value remains weak, stop adding features blindly and reassess the product or reallocate effort to stronger opportunities.
