# PAL Catalog Check — launch metrics contract

## Pre-publication

Track:
- genuine `shopify_beta_interest` events with `is_test=false`
- external merchant/agency replies or legitimate beta requests
- App Store submission readiness gates

Do not count:
- synthetic events
- development-store opens
- development-store scans
- owner/test interactions

## After public App Store availability

Track separately:
- public installs
- successful first scans
- first-scan completion rate when denominator is observable
- repeat scans
- remediation engagement
- uninstall signals where observable and privacy-safe
- explicit commercial/paid-feature interest once such a path exists
- gross app revenue
- processing/platform costs
- net app revenue
- cumulative net revenue after the one-time App Store registration cost

## First-revenue milestone

First revenue is counted only when a real external merchant has been charged successfully through an authorized, implemented billing path.

## Registration break-even milestone

The one-time App Store registration fee is considered recovered only when cumulative observable net PAL Catalog Check app revenue exceeds the actual registration expense after relevant processing/platform costs.

## Decision discipline

Do not confuse technical success with demand.

If exposure is low, improve distribution before judging the product.

If exposure is adequate but installs/activation are weak, improve positioning/onboarding and reassess the pain.

If merchants activate but do not repeat or express willingness to pay for recurring value, avoid adding large paid feature sets blindly.

If recurring usage and commercial intent appear, prioritize the smallest paid recurring capability that captures the demonstrated value.
