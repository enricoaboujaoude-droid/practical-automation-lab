Practical Automation Lab · Digital Product Passport

Check battery-passport data gaps before 18 February 2027.

Choose a battery category, upload a CSV or JSON record, or paste sanitized data. The preflight compares your record with the European Commission's August 2026 battery-passport guidance covering 71 data points and category-specific applicability.

Scope: from 18 February 2027, each EV battery, each LMT battery, and each industrial battery above 2 kWh placed on the EU market or put into service must have a battery passport. This tool checks data readiness only; it does not create a legally valid passport or replace the Batteries Regulation, delegated acts, conformity assessment, or the DPP Registry.
