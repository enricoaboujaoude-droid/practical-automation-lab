Priority checklist

  • Confirm each referral link resolves to the intended vendor landing page.
  • Check whether referral parameters remain intact through redirects.
  • Review links after vendor rebrands, pricing-page moves, or program migrations.
  • Prioritize SaaS review, comparison, and tutorial pages with existing clicks.

How to prioritize repairs

Rank suspected links by traffic × purchase intent × expected commission. Repair the pages most likely to be costing money before low-traffic archive links.
