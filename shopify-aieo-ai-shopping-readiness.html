AIEO · AI shopping · Shopify

SEO helps people find your pages. AIEO helps AI understand your products.

PAL uses AIEO — AI Engine Optimization — as shorthand for making product data clear, structured, complete and current enough for AI shopping assistants and agents to parse, compare and act on. It is not a replacement for SEO. It is the commerce-data layer that traditional SEO does not cover by itself.

Why this matters now

AI channels consume product data

Shopify says eligible products can be syndicated to AI channels through Shopify Catalog with structured titles, descriptions, options, images, pricing and availability.

ChatGPT uses merchant and product metadata

OpenAI says merchant selection and shopping results use product metadata, and approved merchants can provide direct product feeds for current product information.

Google is measuring AI shopping discovery

Google Merchant Center now reports AI-surface performance and recommends improving product data for AI Mode, AI Overviews and Gemini.

Clean data supports action, not only discovery

Agentic commerce needs reliable identifiers, variants, price and availability so an AI system can move from understanding a product to recommending or purchasing it.

What PAL audits for AIEO

Identity

Titles, brand/vendor, GTIN/barcode, MPN/SKU and duplicate identifiers.

Offer clarity

Price, availability, variants, option combinations and live product links.

Visual data

Image presence, URL quality and observable image-dimension readiness.

Machine consistency

Feed structure, field consistency and remediation output that APIs, agents and operations teams can consume.

For stores still focused only on SEO

If your optimization work stops at keywords, metadata and page ranking, add a catalog-data pass. AI shopping systems increasingly consume structured commerce data directly. The practical upgrade is not to abandon SEO; it is to make the source catalog explicit enough that both search engines and AI systems receive accurate product facts.

PAL's position: SEO + AIEO is stronger than SEO-only for commerce because the two solve different problems: discovery of pages versus machine understanding of products.

Start with the lowest-friction path
