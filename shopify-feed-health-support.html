PAL Catalog Check support

PAL Catalog Check is a read-only Shopify catalog-readiness scanner. It reviews catalog fields and highlights issues that can make product-feed preparation harder. It does not edit products or guarantee approval by Google Merchant Center or any other sales channel.

What the scan checks

The current scan reviews product titles, vendor/brand presence, Online Store URLs, images, image dimensions when Shopify provides them, variant identifiers, variant prices, and duplicate variant-option combinations.

Scan boundaries

A scan checks up to 2,500 products, up to 100 variants per product, and up to 20 images per product. The embedded app shows a warning when the product or variant boundary is reached so merchants do not mistake a partial scan for a complete one.

Permissions

The app requests only Shopify's read_products scope for its core catalog scan. It does not request customer or order data and it does not write to products or variants.

If the app does not load

First reopen the app from Shopify Admin → Apps. If the embedded app still shows an application error, record the approximate time and the visible error message. Do not include access tokens, passwords, recovery codes, or customer information.

If the scan reports an unexpected issue

Include the issue code, the affected product title, and what field you expected the app to read. Avoid posting confidential catalog exports or credentials. The scan is intentionally conservative and does not invent product identifiers.

Privacy

See the dedicated PAL Catalog Check privacy policy for Shopify data-access, session-storage, logging, and deletion details.

Uninstalling

You can remove the app from Shopify Admin through the store's app settings. Shopify sends uninstall and later shop-redaction events; the app uses those events to remove persisted installation-session data for that shop.
