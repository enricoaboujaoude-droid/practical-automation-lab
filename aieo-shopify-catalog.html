If your catalog is optimized only for traditional SEO, PAL checks the missing AI layer.

Product identity

Consistent titles, brand/vendor, GTIN, MPN/SKU and canonical product URLs help agents identify the exact product instead of guessing.

Variant clarity

Unique option combinations, sizes, colors and variant identifiers reduce ambiguity when an AI agent compares close alternatives.

Price & availability

Clean price formatting and current availability improve machine comparison and reduce recommendations for products that cannot actually be bought.

Images & attributes

Strong images plus complete material, dimensions, use case and technical attributes give AI systems more evidence to match products to natural-language requests.

PAL AIEO workflow

  1. Detect: scan catalog/feed fields that AI and commerce systems depend on.
  2. Prioritize: separate identity, variant, image, price, availability and machine-readability issues.
  3. Remediate: generate a structured correction plan instead of generic SEO advice.
  4. Monitor: re-check the catalog as products and variants change.
