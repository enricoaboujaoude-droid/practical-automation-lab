# Practical Automation Lab — AI Commerce Discovery

Practical Automation Lab provides AIEO (AI Engine Optimization) and commerce-data readiness tools.

AIEO is PAL terminology for improving the clarity, structure, completeness and freshness of product data so AI shopping assistants and agents can parse products reliably. It complements SEO; it does not guarantee ranking or inclusion on any AI channel.

## Primary merchant offer

PAL Catalog Check for Shopify
https://apps.shopify.com/pal-catalog-check

Free read-only scan of Shopify product data for AI-commerce and feed readiness:
- titles and vendor/brand
- product URLs
- images and observable dimensions
- GTIN/barcode and SKU/MPN identifiers
- price
- variant structure
- catalog ambiguity
